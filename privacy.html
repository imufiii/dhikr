Privacy Policy

Your dhikr is private.

Last updated: June 2026

We are committed to your privacy.
No account required. Your data stays on your device.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-up required
  • No analytics or usage data sent to external servers
  • No crash reports sent to external servers
  • No user profiling or behavioral tracking

What stays on your device

The app stores the following locally — it never leaves your phone:

  • Your dhikr counts and daily totals
  • Custom phrases you add
  • App settings (haptic, shake, targets)
  • Your approximate location for prayer times

Location

Location is requested only to calculate local prayer times, using the open-source Adhan library entirely on your device. Your coordinates are never sent to any server. If you decline, the app uses Mecca as a default.

Notifications

Prayer time notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved in delivering them.

Children

Dhikr does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
