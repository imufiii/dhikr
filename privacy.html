Privacy Policy

Last updated:June 2026

No data leaves your device

Dhikr does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores — your dhikr history, prayer counts, settings, and custom phrases — is saved locally on your device only.

Location

Dhikr requests your location solely to calculate local prayer times. This calculation happens entirely on your device. Your coordinates are never sent to any server.

No accounts, no tracking

Dhikr requires no account, login, or registration. We use no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.
